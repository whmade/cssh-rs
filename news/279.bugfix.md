A client's existing colored output is now preserved when its state
changes. Previously, disabling and re-enabling a client, or moving the
control-mode submenu highlight over it, repainted the whole console with
a single uniform color and destroyed the per-cell colors of text that
was already on screen (a colored prompt, `ls --color`, program output).
The client now snapshots those colors before tinting the window and
restores them when it returns to the enabled state.
