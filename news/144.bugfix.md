Pressing `Ctrl + C` or `Ctrl + Break` while the daemon is focused now
interrupts each client's child process instead of inserting a literal
`^C` at the prompt. Previously these key presses were replayed as plain
input, so programs reading cooked input (e.g. `cmd.exe`, or `ssh` at a
local password prompt) showed an editable `^C` and were never
interrupted; raw sessions that want the literal byte are unaffected.
